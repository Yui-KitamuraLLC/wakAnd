Privacy Policy for wakAnd

wakAnd ("the App") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect and use information.

1. Information Collection and Use
To enable its core functionality of tracking detailed app/browser activity and syncing with WakaTime, the App uses the Accessibility Service API.

- App Info and Browser URLs: The App collects the names of running apps and the URLs of websites you visit while using browsers (such as Chrome).
- Usage Timing: The App records the duration spent on these apps or URLs.
- Background Collection: This information is collected even when the App is closed or not in use, provided the Accessibility Service is enabled by the user.

2. Purpose of Collection
The collected information is used solely for:
- Calculating time spent on specific apps, websites, or domains.
- Syncing this activity data with your WakaTime account via the WakaTime API.

3. Data Sharing and Disclosure
- The App sends the collected app names, URLs and duration data to WakaTime (wakatime.com) or your configured custom API endpoint.
- We do NOT share, sell, or use this information for advertising or any other third-party purposes.
- Data is transmitted securely via HTTPS.

4. User Control and Consent
- Accessibility Service: The use of the Accessibility Service API is optional and requires explicit consent through a disclosure dialog and system settings. You can disable this at any time in the App or Android system settings.
- Usage Access: The App may also request "Usage Access" to track time spent in other Android applications.

5. Data Retention
The App does not maintain a permanent local database of your browsing history; it primarily acts as a bridge to send data to WakaTime.

6. Changes to This Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes.

7. Contact Us
If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer.
